# RoadCue Zero-Budget Organic Launch Campaign

Campaign window: 2026-08-27 to 2026-09-26  
Budget: CAD $0  
Primary market: Canada  
Campaign anchor: [Meet RoadCue: Direction-Aware Camera Alerts for Canadian Drivers](https://medium.com/@kevinmok/meet-roadcue-direction-aware-camera-alerts-for-canadian-drivers-889148ebf2de)

## Campaign Objective

Introduce RoadCue as a focused Canadian driving companion, turn the launch story into reusable content, establish the first trustworthy ratings and community conversations, and learn which free channels produce qualified App Store downloads.

## Core Story

**Most camera-alert apps can show where cameras are, but alerts may require an entered destination and active navigation. RoadCue provides spoken alerts for listed fixed cameras without requiring a route, identifies the listed camera type, and runs quietly beside the navigation, rideshare, or delivery app a Canadian driver already uses.**

Supporting proof:

- Available now for iPhone and free to use.
- 914 reviewed fixed-camera records in the current build.
- Matching is processed on the device.
- No account, advertising, analytics, or subscription.
- Interface and spoken guidance in nine languages.
- Honest limitations: RoadCue does not detect radar, police, or mobile photo-radar units and does not replace signs or traffic laws.

Most camera-alert apps can tell you where cameras are.

That wasn't the problem I wanted to solve.

The frustration was having to enter a destination and start navigation before alerts would work—even when I already knew where I was going or needed another driving app in front.

I also found myself looking for roadside signs to determine whether a location had only a red-light camera or a combined red-light and speed camera. Sometimes the cameras weren't obvious either.

That's attention I would rather keep on the road ahead.

So I built RoadCue.

RoadCue runs quietly in the background without requiring you to start a route. It provides spoken alerts for listed fixed cameras, identifies the camera type, and works alongside the navigation, rideshare, or delivery app you already use.

It's free, with no account, advertising, analytics, or subscription.
