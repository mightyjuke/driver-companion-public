STAYS IN ITS LANE.

RoadCue is not another map to manage. Start driving once, then keep the app you already rely on in front.

  • Background audio

    Hear the relevant warning while navigation, delivery, or rideshare stays open.

  • One-tap driving mode

    A glanceable status, live speed and heading, and one primary action.

  • Flexible warning distance

    Choose when the first spoken alert plays; the final approach tone stays fixed.

CANADIAN SOURCES.
HONEST LIMITS.

RoadCue’s build-time database is assembled from reviewed provincial and municipal sources. Coverage depends on what each jurisdiction publishes and is not complete or live-updated everywhere.

What RoadCue does not claim

RoadCue does not detect radar signals, police activity, mobile photo-radar units, or guarantee that every fixed camera is listed. It is not a government app. Always follow posted signs and traffic laws.
