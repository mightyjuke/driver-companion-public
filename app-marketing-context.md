## Value Proposition

- **Problem:** Drivers may want fixed-camera awareness without entering a destination, starting turn-by-turn navigation, keeping a particular navigation app active, or remembering to start a separate alert session for every trip.
- **Target Audience:** Canadian drivers, especially rideshare and delivery drivers and people who want to keep Apple Maps, Google Maps, a vehicle navigation system, or another driving app in the foreground.
- **Unique Differentiator:** RoadCue is a destination-free background companion with optional Automatic Protection: it can detect sustained driving, begin monitoring automatically, and stop after 10 continuous stationary minutes. It requires no screen overlay, while on-device distance-and-direction matching helps keep alerts relevant beside the driver's preferred app.
- **Elevator Pitch:** RoadCue is a quiet Canadian camera-warning companion that can start automatically and work beside the driving apps people already use—without a destination or active route.

## Priority Audience Segments

1. Rideshare and delivery drivers who keep a work app or navigation app in the foreground and do not want a separate route running for camera alerts.
2. Daily commuters and familiar-route drivers who want Automatic Protection to begin after sustained driving without remembering to start a session.
3. Privacy-conscious drivers who value on-device processing, no account, no advertising, and no analytics.
4. Canadian drivers who benefit from interface and spoken guidance in languages beyond English and French.

## Message Pillars

1. **No destination or active route:** RoadCue provides listed fixed-camera awareness without requiring turn-by-turn navigation.
2. **Automatic Protection:** With the optional setting and Always location access, RoadCue starts after sustained driving and stops after 10 continuous stationary minutes.
3. **Works beside existing apps:** No screen overlay is required; navigation, rideshare, and delivery apps can remain in front.
4. **Direction improves relevance:** A camera can be nearby without being relevant, so RoadCue evaluates both distance and movement direction.
5. **Privacy is architectural:** Matching runs on the phone with no account, advertising, or analytics pipeline.
6. **Built for Canadian drivers:** The current build contains 914 reviewed records, while the interface and spoken guidance support nine languages.

## Claims Guardrails

- Say "direction-aware" or "designed to reject" rather than claiming perfect direction detection.
- Describe Automatic Protection as optional, say it starts after sustained driving, and state that it requires Always location access and may use more battery.
- Say it stops after 10 continuous stationary minutes; do not shorten this to a vague promise that it always knows when a trip has ended.
- Do not imply monitoring survives force-quitting or operating-system termination. Force-quitting stops background location, and Android restart behavior varies by manufacturer.
- Say "listed fixed cameras" rather than implying complete or live camera coverage.
- Never claim that RoadCue prevents tickets, detects radar, detects police, or replaces signs and traffic laws.
- State that RoadCue is not affiliated with a government or enforcement agency.
- Do not call RoadCue a navigation replacement.
- Do not invent ratings, testimonials, usage totals, press coverage, or download milestones.
