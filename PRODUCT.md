## Platform

## Stack

Delegated: a dependency-light static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site, chosen for fast GitHub Pages deployment, low maintenance, and strong crawlability.

## Users

Canadian drivers—especially delivery, rideshare, and navigation-app users—who want timely fixed-camera awareness without replacing the driving apps they already use.

## Product Purpose

RoadCue is a focused companion app that monitors a driver's direction and distance on-device and gives audio warnings when the route is closing on a listed red-light or speed-camera intersection. Success means the driver can keep their preferred navigation, rideshare, or delivery app in the foreground while RoadCue provides calm, glanceable camera awareness.

## Positioning

RoadCue is the quiet, privacy-friendly camera-warning companion built to work beside existing driving apps. Its direction-aware approach is designed to reject cameras behind the driver and on nearby parallel roads, while all detection runs on the device.

## Operating Context

Drivers start a single driving mode, grant precise location access, and receive background voice or sound warnings during a trip. The app shows live speed, heading, the nearest relevant camera, and monitoring status. It supports English, French, Punjabi, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Tagalog, Arabic, and Hindi.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Direction-aware alerts for listed red-light, speed, and combined-camera locations.
- Background audio and voice warnings, with a crossing confirmation after the camera is passed.
- A reviewed, build-time snapshot of official Canadian sources containing 914 records in the current repository.
- No account, advertising, analytics, subscription, or remote detection service.
- RoadCue uses a location database; it does not detect radar signals, guarantee every camera is listed, or replace posted signs and traffic laws.
- It is not a government app. Coverage depends on what provinces and municipalities publish and is not updated live on the device.
- The App Store product URL and public release date are not yet present in the repository and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is RoadCue. Preserve the current camera-location signal icon and the app's signal-first transport-signage character: calm, authoritative, precise, and privacy-forward. The previous SignalSafe name is retired. The website should feel modern and technically credible without police, radar-gun, map-pin clutter, or fear-based messaging.

## Product Principles

- Warn only when the available route evidence says the camera is relevant.
- Keep attention on the road and interaction demands low.
- Make privacy a product behavior, not a slogan.
- Be precise about data coverage and technical limitations.
- Complement the driver's existing tools instead of trying to replace them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The marketing site must meet WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals, remain fully usable by keyboard, respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve strong text contrast, and support narrow mobile screens. Product language support should be stated accurately without implying that the marketing site itself is fully localized.
