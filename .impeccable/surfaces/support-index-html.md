# RoadCue Support

- Scope: `/support/`; Read mode.
- Audience: RoadCue 1.2.0 users resolving alert, audio, location, and background-monitoring issues while safely parked.
- Job: Diagnose the visible symptom, restore the correct iOS or Android permission, or submit a precise camera-data correction.
- Primary action: Email `support@roadcue.ca`; secondary actions are issue-first troubleshooting and the prepared correction email.
- Evidence: The verified 1.2.0 permission and tracking implementation, current 914-record build-time snapshot, and documented audio/background limitations.
- Constraints: Never imply live camera updates, complete coverage, radar detection, emergency response, or that troubleshooting should happen while driving.
- Direction: A support control deck in the established RoadCue instrument world, with ruled diagnostic rows instead of a generic FAQ-card grid.
- Memorable moment: The first viewport aligns the four readiness signals—location services, precise location, background access, and audible media—beside the direct support address.
- Route strategy: Move from symptom to remedy in one corridor: issue-first disclosures, a bone permission field split by platform, a structured correction-intake checklist, then a signal-red contact close.
- Navigation: A compact four-stop jump band stays sticky on desktop for troubleshooting, permissions, camera data, and contact; it becomes a static two-by-two index on narrow screens so it does not consume the reading viewport.
- Interaction: Keep the first troubleshooting disclosure open as the fastest starting point, use native `details` rows for the rest, and make both support paths real `mailto:` actions; the correction action pre-fills only the evidence needed to investigate a record.
- Unresolved: `support@roadcue.ca` needs a real mailbox or forwarding route before publication.
